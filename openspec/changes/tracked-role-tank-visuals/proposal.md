# Proposal

## Why

The current geometric Tank and Damagedealer and round Healer do not read strongly enough as tanks. The user selected H1 Sanitaetspanzer, T2 Bastion and D1 Jaeger from nine visual studies and additionally requested a wrench that actually resembles the tool throughout the game.

## What Changes

- Replace all three role drawings with tracked, armored vehicles using the selected designs in gameplay and lobby.
- Rotate each cannon toward its combat target independently of the chassis.
- Replace all wrench drawings with one recognizable combination wrench: open jaw, solid shaft and open ring end.
- Preserve team identification, selection, ability effects, camera follow and gameplay rules.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `role-visual-distinction`: Require tracked role-specific tanks, independent aiming and consistent tool-shaped repair iconography.

## Impact

Frontend Phaser drawings, lobby previews, ability HUD and healing indicators, renderer tests and related documentation. No balance, networking protocol or dependency changes.
